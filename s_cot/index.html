Spectral-R1

Latent Energy-Based GRPO Reasoning

Teaching small models to reason about graphs through spectral energy alignment

Home Dashboard Results Method GitHub

Spectral-R1 trains compact language models to solve graph reasoning tasks by aligning latent representations with spectral energy objectives. Using a Contextual Hamiltonian formulation and Rayleigh Conjugate Descent, the model learns structured reasoning over weighted graphs -- achieving significant accuracy gains with shorter completions. Target venue: NeurIPS 2025.
